Selected work

Styles

  • Black and grey realism

    Smooth shading and sharp contrast for portraits, hands and religious imagery, planned to stay readable as it heals.

  • Fine line and illustrative

    Delicate linework for birds, snakes, lettering and small symbols, with the occasional touch of red.

  • Brushstroke and geometric

    Ink-wash dragons and koi, plus clean geometric shapes drawn to sit well on the body.

The artist

Marco Vale has tattooed for twelve years and opened Sick Ink to work one-on-one with clients. Known for black and grey realism, fine line work and ink-wash brushstroke pieces, every design is drawn from scratch.

Each project starts with a conversation about your idea, the placement and how the tattoo will age.

Questions

How do I book?

Start with the consultation form. It goes straight to the artist, who replies using the contact detail you gave.

Do you take walk-ins?

Walk-ins are accepted when there is space. Messaging on Signal first is the safest way to get a time.

How much does a tattoo cost?

Price depends on size, detail and time. You get a quote after the consultation, before you commit.

Do I need to pay a deposit?

A deposit holds your date and counts toward the final price.

Can I get a cover-up?

Yes. Send clear photos of the existing tattoo in Signal and the artist will tell you what is realistic.
